IThe Service

Veyo produces institutional readings of brands and creator-led brands: a rating, forward signals, and a standing on the Veyo Index, generated from publicly available information and reviewed under Veyo's evaluation discipline. The complimentary reading is a preview of the first layer of that discipline.

IIThe Nature of a Reading

A reading is analysis, not advice. It is produced with the assistance of automated systems from public-surface information, and it reflects what could be observed at the time of the reading. It is directional by design. It is not financial, legal, investment, or professional advice, and no decision should rest on a reading alone. Where a figure could not be verified, the reading says so rather than inventing one; where it errs, tell us, and the archive corrects.

IIIThe Archive

Readings are retained in the Veyo Benchmark Archive. Evidence from every reading may be used in aggregated, anonymized form for benchmarking and the construction of the Veyo Index. Nothing leaves. This is how every reading deepens the next one, for everyone.
