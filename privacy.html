Privacy Policy

Effective July 2026 · Stockholm, Sweden

IWho We Are

Veyo is an institutional intelligence practice based in Stockholm, Sweden. For anything in this policy, write to hello@veyo.house. Discretion is not a feature of the house; it is the house.

IIWhat We Collect, and Why

Your correspondence. If you request the complete dossier, we collect the email address you provide, in order to deliver it and to respond when you write. Legal basis: performance of what you asked for.

Your consent, separately. Updates about Veyo readings and the Index are sent only if you tick the box that asks. You may withdraw at any time by writing to us or using the unsubscribe link; withdrawal changes nothing about what you already received.

Operational records. The instrument keeps minimal technical logs of readings performed: the subject requested, the time, and whether the reading completed. These protect the service from abuse and tell us what the market is curious about. Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating and securing the instrument.

We do not sell personal data. We do not run advertising. There are no trackers here beyond what is strictly needed for the pages to function.

IIIThe Subjects of Readings

Readings analyze brands and creator-led brands using publicly available information: what a house or a creator has chosen to place in public view. Where a subject is an individual, we process only public-domain professional information, on the basis of legitimate interest in market analysis, and we state sources honestly, mark estimates as estimates, and write "insufficient public data" rather than inventing. If you are the subject of a reading and wish to object, correct, or inquire, write to us; the archive corrects.

IVThe Archive

Readings are retained in the Veyo Benchmark Archive and may be used in aggregated, anonymized form for benchmarking and index construction. Your email address never enters the archive; correspondence and evidence live in separate rooms.

VWhere Data Lives

Veyo runs on a small set of infrastructure partners for hosting, storage, and analysis, each bound by their own data-processing commitments, with data held in the EU or under recognized transfer safeguards. We keep personal data only as long as its purpose stands: correspondence for as long as our relationship does, consent until withdrawn, operational logs briefly.

VIYour Rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing, and you may complain to your supervisory authority (in Sweden, IMY). One letter to hello@veyo.house is enough; we answer without theater.
